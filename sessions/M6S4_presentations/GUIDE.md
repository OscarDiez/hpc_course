# M6S4 · Final group project presentation (syllabus Session 29)

**Purpose:** Show what your team built or studied, what you measured, and what you learned. Follow the project rules already communicated; this document clarifies the *presentation*, not a new assignment.

## Suggested format

- **7 minutes** presentation + **3 minutes** questions per group (adapt to the number of groups and available time; teacher will confirm).
- Every member should contribute. Show a reproducible result or concrete evidence, not just a proposal.
- Slides: ideally **5–7**, maximum 5 bullets per slide. Include one diagram, table or graph only when it teaches something.

## Required content (six parts)

1. **Problem and objective:** What question did you address and why might HPC help?
2. **Baseline:** What is the sequential or initial method? What did it cost?
3. **Parallel / technical approach:** MPI, OpenMP, GPU, workflow or comparative analysis; diagram the actual data/work split.
4. **Evidence:** Show a table or plot of your real results, configuration, number of cores/ranks and units. Avoid unsupported speedup claims.
5. **Bottleneck and limitations:** What did not improve? What are the correctness and reproducibility limits?
6. **Conclusion:** What would you change next, and why?

## Presentation rubric (the existing 3% group-work presentation component)

| Criterion | Weight within presentation | What earns full credit |
|---|---:|---|
| Clarity and technical correctness | 25% | Accurate terms and a clear problem statement |
| HPC design and explanation | 25% | Correct allocation, dependencies and choice of tools |
| Results and evidence | 25% | Reproducible measurements/artefacts, good units and baseline |
| Critical analysis | 15% | Explains bottlenecks, constraints and limitations honestly |
| Communication and team contribution | 10% | Clear slides, within time, able to answer questions |

**Important:** This proposed rubric should be matched to any previously published rubric. It does not change the syllabus weights: project plan 3%, milestone report 4%, final output 5%, presentation 3%.

**Student checklist:** Bring slides in PDF/PPTX, project repository or reproducible evidence, and one clear answer to “Where did the time go?”
