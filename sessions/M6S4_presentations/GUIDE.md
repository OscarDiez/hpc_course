# M6S4 · Final group project presentation (syllabus Session 29)

**Purpose:** Show what your team built or studied, what you measured, and what you learned. Follow the project rules already communicated; this document clarifies the *presentation*, not a new assignment.

## Format already published in Blackboard

- **5-minute presentation** with approximately **5 slides** per group, as specified in the existing Group Assignment instructions on Blackboard.
- Every member should contribute. Show a reproducible result or concrete evidence, not just a proposal.
- Use at most five clear bullets per slide; prefer one relevant result graph or table to large amounts of text.

## Five-slide structure from the existing assignment

1. **Problem and application:** What did you compute and why is HPC useful?
2. **Architecture and technologies:** Show the actual HPC design and approaches you tested.
3. **Performance and scaling:** Give the baseline and measured results, with units and resources.
4. **Bottleneck and optimization:** Explain what limited performance, what you changed and whether it helped.
5. **Lessons and EuroHPC:** State limitations, what you would improve, and how your work could scale to a larger machine.

## Assessment

The **presentation** counts for **3% of the final course grade**, within the existing 15% group-work assessment in the official syllabus. This guide introduces **no new marking rubric** and does not replace the requirements in Blackboard. Present technically correct, reproducible evidence and explain your findings clearly.

**Student checklist:** Bring slides in PDF/PPTX, project repository or reproducible evidence, and one clear answer to “Where did the time go?”
